Our paper *Formal Analysis of Multi-Factor Authentication Schemes in Digital Identity Cards*, by Matteo Paier, Roberto Van Eeden and Marino Miculan, has received the **Best Paper Award** at the [22nd International Conference on Software Engineering and Formal Methods (SEFM 2024)](https://sefm-conference.github.io/2024/)! 🏆

The [associated artifact](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.12586055) has also received both the *Reusable* and the *Available* [badges of EAPLS](https://eapls.org/pages/artifact_badges/).
