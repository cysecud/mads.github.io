At [TYPES 2026](https://types2026.cse.chalmers.se/) in Gothenburg it has been announced that the next edition, **TYPES 2027**, will take place in **Udine**, organised by our lab.

TYPES is the oldest conference on all aspects of type theory and its applications, and the lab has a long tradition in this community: Udine already hosted TYPES 2007 in Cividale del Friuli. Marino Miculan has also joined the Steering Committee of the conference. Stay tuned for the call for contributions!
