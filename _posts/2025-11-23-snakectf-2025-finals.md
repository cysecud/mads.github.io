The international finals of [snakeCTF 2025](https://snakectf.org/editions/2025-finals), the cybersecurity competition organised by our student team [MadrHacks](https://madrhacks.org), closed today in Lignano Sabbiadoro.

For four days, 15 elite teams from 10 countries competed on challenges crafted by MadrHacks. The podium:

- 🥇 [BunkyoWesterns](https://bunkyowesterns.org/) 🇯🇵
- 🥈 [TheRomanXpl0it](https://theromanxpl0.it/) 🇮🇹
- 🥉 [Superflat](https://superfl.at/) 🇳🇱

The complete ranking is on [CTFtime](https://ctftime.org/event/2994). Congratulations to all the teams, and many thanks to MadrHacks for the impeccable organisation. Keep hacking 🐍!
