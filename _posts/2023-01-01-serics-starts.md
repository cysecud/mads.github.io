Today the project [SERICS (SEcurity and RIghts in the CyberSpace)](https://serics.eu) begins! SERICS is one of the [14 partnerships selected by the Ministry](https://www.mur.gov.it/it/news/mercoledi-03082022/pnrr-mur-selezionati-i-14-partenariati-attivita-di-ricerca) for advanced research within the PNRR, on the theme *Cybersecurity, new technologies and rights protection*.

The lab takes part in Spoke 6, *Software and Platform Security*, led by Ca' Foscari University of Venice, working in particular on the projects [SWOPS and SECCO](https://serics.eu/progetti/).
