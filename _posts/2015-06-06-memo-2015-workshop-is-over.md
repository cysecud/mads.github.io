The [Second International Workshop on Meta Models for processes](http://discotec2015.inria.fr/memo-2015/) has been held in Grenoble, organized also by the MADSLab and associated to DisCoTec 2015. The meeting has been quite successful, with a cool invited talk by Michele Loreti and several interesting contributions. Thanks to all the authors and participants for a vibrant and lively discussion!

The pre-proceedings of the meeting are [available online](https://webconf.inrialpes.fr/wp-content/uploads/sites/9/2015/06/memo2015-preproc.pdf). Soon we will publish the deadlines for the JLAMP special issue. Stay tuned!
