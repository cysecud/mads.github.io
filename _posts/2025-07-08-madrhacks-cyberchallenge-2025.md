Congratulations to the MadrHacks team of the University of Udine for their performance at the [CyberChallenge.IT 2025 national final](https://cyberchallenge.it/attack-defense/2025)! Facing 39 teams from all over Italy, they reached **11th place**, the best result of any team from North-East Italy.

Well done to Mattia Calligaro, Daniel Fragrante, Gabriele Tommasi, Michele Vecchiato, Nicolò Frescura and Oliver Stevanovic. *Sicut Serpentes!* 🐍
