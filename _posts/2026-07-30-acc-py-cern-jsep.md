Our paper *Security Assessment of Private Package Repositories: An Experience on Acc-Py at CERN*, by Michele Lizzit, Francesco Pinzauti, Marino Miculan and Vincenzo Riccio, has been published (open access) in the [Journal of Software: Evolution and Process](https://doi.org/10.1002/smr.70159) (Wiley), vol. 38, no. 8. [\[pdf\]](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/pdf/10.1002/smr.70159)

The paper reports on a security assessment of Acc-Py, the private Python package repository used at CERN, and is a joint work of our Cybersecurity and Testing working groups.
