Our paper *Formal modelling and verifying eIDAS multi-factor authentication with interface-based threat analysis*, by Matteo Paier, Roberto Van Eeden and Marino Miculan, has been published in [Software and Systems Modeling](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10270-026-01375-9) (Springer).

The paper extends our previous work on the formal analysis of multi-factor authentication in digital identity cards, which received the [Best Paper Award at SEFM 2024]({{ site.baseurl }}/2024/11/sefm24-best-paper/).
