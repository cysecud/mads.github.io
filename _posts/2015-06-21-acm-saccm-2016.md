The [Special Track on Coordination Models, Languages and Applications](http://sac2016.apice.unibo.it) of the ACM SAC 2016 conference is devoted to models, formalisms and mechanisms to describe concurrent and distributed computations. Submission deadline is Sep 11, 2015. See [here](http://sac2016.apice.unibo.it/cfp.html) for the complete call for papers.
