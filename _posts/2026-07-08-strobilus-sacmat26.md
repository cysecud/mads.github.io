Our paper *Strobilus: Enriching Cedar with Stateful Policies*, by Massimiliano Baldo, Pietro Di Gianantonio, Matteo Paier and Marino Miculan, has been published in the [ACM Digital Library](https://doi.org/10.1145/3750555.3811892) as part of the proceedings of [SACMAT 2026](https://www.sacmat.org/2026/), the ACM Symposium on Access Control Models and Technologies.

Strobilus extends the Cedar policy language with stateful policies. [\[pdf\]](https://marino.miculan.org/assets/pdf/2026-SACMAT.pdf) [\[artifact\]](https://github.com/strobilus-lang/strobilus-tinytodo-example)
