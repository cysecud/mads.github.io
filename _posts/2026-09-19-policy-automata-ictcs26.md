From September 7th to 9th Udine hosted [ICTCS 2026](https://ictcs2026.uniud.it/), the Italian Conference on Theoretical Computer Science, where we presented our paper *Policy Automata for Stateful Authorization*, by Massimiliano Baldo, Matteo Paier and Marino Miculan.

The paper is now published in the [proceedings](https://ceur-ws.org/Vol-4269/) of the conference (CEUR-WS). [\[pdf\]](https://ceur-ws.org/Vol-4269/paper45.pdf)
