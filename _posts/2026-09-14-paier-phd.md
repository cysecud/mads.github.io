Today Matteo Paier successfully defended his PhD thesis, *Fine-grained symbolic verification of heterogeneous systems*, at IMT Lucca, receiving an *excellent* evaluation. The Commission was composed of professors Luca Viganò, Chiara Bodei and Gabriele Costa.

Matteo is now a PhD in Cybersecurity. Congratulations!
