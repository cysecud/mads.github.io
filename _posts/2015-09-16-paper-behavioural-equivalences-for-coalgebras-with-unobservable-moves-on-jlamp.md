The paper [*Behavioural equivalences for coalgebras with unobservable moves*](http://dx.doi.org/10.1016/j.jlamp.2015.09.002), by Tomasz Brengos and MADSLab members Marino Miculan and Marco Peressotti, has been published on the Special Issue of the *Journal of Logical and Algebraic Methods in Programming* dedicated to the [OPCT 2014 Research Seminar](http://opct2014.cs.vu.nl).
