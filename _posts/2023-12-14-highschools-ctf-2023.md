On December 12th, more than 150 students from secondary schools in Udine and Gemona took part in the fifth **HighSchools CTF Workshop** at the University of Udine. The event is part of the [CyberHighSchools](https://cyberhighschools.it) programme of the [Cybersecurity National Lab](https://cybersecnatlab.it), dedicated to students and teachers of secondary schools, under the patronage of the [National Cybersecurity Agency](https://acn.gov.it) and the [Italian Data Protection Authority](https://www.garanteprivacy.it).

The event was organised locally by the MADS lab and [MadrHacks](https://www.madrhacks.org). GG MadrHacks 🐍!
