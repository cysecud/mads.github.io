Our research project **STENDHAL** (*Spatio-Temporal Enhancement of Neural Nets for Deeply Hierarchical Automatised Logic*) has been approved under the PRIN 2022 programme. The project is led by the University of Pisa and includes the University of Udine (with Marino Miculan as local Principal Investigator) and ISTI-CNR.

STENDHAL brings spatio-temporal capabilities to the analysis of image sequences, enabling video analysis and longitudinal studies in healthcare, and integrates spatial model checking with neural networks. Its methods will be validated on case studies in the healthcare and cyber-physical domains.
