[MadrHacks](https://www.madrhacks.org), the ethical hacking team of the University of Udine, won the second edition of [CyberCup](https://cybercup.it), the national cybersecurity tournament held from October 2023 to July 2024. They beat about 20 teams from renowned universities (see the [ranking](https://cybercup.it/scoreboard.html)).

The trophy was awarded in Turin on July 5th, during the national finals of [CyberChallenge.IT 2024](https://cyberchallenge.it). Here is the official [press release](https://qui.uniud.it/ateneo/lateneo-di-udine-vince-la-cybercup-il-campionato-nazionale-di-cybersicurezza/). Go MadrHacks 🐍!
