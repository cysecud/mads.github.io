The paper *Open Transactions on Shared Memory*, by MADSLab members Marino Miculan, Marco Peressotti, and Andrea Toneguzzo, have been presented at [COORDINATION 2015](http://discotec2015.inria.fr/program/), the conference on *Coordination Models and Languages* part of the DisCoTec, the International Federated Conference on Distributed Computing Techniques. Thanks to the many participants who have asked questions, raised remarks and fostered a very interesting discussion!

Now we are working on the effective implementation… stay tuned!
